The following are design and positioning decisions drawn from the inspected references and the user's requirements, rather than invented research findings.

## What the visitor needs to decide

A visitor is hiring an individual to improve a Twitch channel. The page should answer: Who is Emmanuel? What can I hire him for? Does his visual direction fit me? What exactly might be delivered? How do I start a conversation?

The previous page emphasized an automated assessment, an interactive blueprint, and a dashboard-style hero. Those implied a product experience and obscured the person doing the work. They have been removed.

## Structure and rationale

- **Hero:** First-person service statement and an actual portrait in a purple-ringed circle. Two destinations: a personal inquiry and the work gallery. No dashboard, trial, pricing tiers, fabricated availability, or performance statistics.
- **Capability strip:** Describes actual types of help instead of replacing reference-image statistics with unsupported numbers.
- **About:** Portrait on the left, first-person introduction and practical collaboration expectations on the right. The portrait caption describes working principles rather than supposed past success.
- **Client fit:** Immediately after About. Welcomes new streamers as well as established creators, and makes the boundary against bought engagement, copying, and guaranteed results clear.
- **Services:** A concise introduction and individual service cards inspired by the fifth image. Scope details lead to a preselected inquiry, not a product checkout or subscription.
- **Work:** Larger visuals than the previous agency gallery. A mix of banner, identity, overlay, scene, and panel concepts shows relevant Twitch deliverables. Details explain the creative decisions and scope. All six remain labeled personal concepts, never client case studies.
- **Process:** Four plain steps describing a direct creative collaboration. No interactive growth system or implied software product.
- **Contact:** A project conversation with the real public contact email from the reference site. The form opens a mail draft and explicitly requires the visitor to send it. No fake submission success or automated audit.

## Twitch-specific service language

Prioritize channel banners, profile presentation, overlays, alerts, stream scenes, channel panels, relevant categories/tags, content direction, and promotion planning. Avoid translating ecommerce services into Twitch claims: no CRO, revenue systems, funnel automation, Shopify services, fixed monetization claims, or invented partnerships. Channel optimization describes presentation and discovery guidance rather than guaranteed SEO rankings. Promotion strategy does not quietly imply paid campaign management or guaranteed viewers.

## Visual direction

Near-black surfaces, a subtle dot grid, controlled purple-to-blue accents, rounded portrait and buttons, clean Inter typography, and restrained motion. Original mockups use fictional identities and abstract artwork rather than copyrighted game characters. The person's portrait anchors both the hero and about section. Mobile layouts retain the person, work, and contact path without horizontal overflow.
